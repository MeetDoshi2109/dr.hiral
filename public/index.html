add
A holistic system of medicine based on the principle of 'like cures like', treating the individual as a whole. It seeks to stimulate the body's innate healing mechanisms rather than merely suppressing symptoms.

How long does treatment take?

add
The duration of treatment varies depending on the nature of your illness—whether it is acute or chronic. Acute conditions can resolve quickly, while chronic conditions take longer as homeopathy aims to cure from the root.

Are there any side effects?

add
Homeopathic medicines are highly diluted and therefore completely non-toxic. They are generally considered safe and do not produce harmful side effects when prescribed properly by a qualified homeopath.

Can I take homeopathy with allopathic medicine?

add
Yes, homeopathic medicines can generally be taken alongside conventional treatments without adverse interactions. However, it is important to inform your homeopath about any medications you are currently taking.
