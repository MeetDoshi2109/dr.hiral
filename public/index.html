Understanding classical homeopathy

What is classical homeopathy?

expand_more
A holistic system of medicine based on 'like cures like', treating the individual as a whole. It stimulates the body's innate healing mechanisms rather than suppressing symptoms.

How long does treatment take?

expand_more
Duration varies — acute conditions resolve quickly while chronic conditions take longer as homeopathy aims to cure from the root.

Are there any side effects?

expand_more
Homeopathic medicines are highly diluted and non-toxic. They are safe and do not produce harmful side effects when prescribed by a qualified homeopath.

Can I take homeopathy with allopathic medicine?

expand_more
Yes, homeopathic medicines can be taken alongside conventional treatments without adverse interactions. Inform your homeopath about current medications.
