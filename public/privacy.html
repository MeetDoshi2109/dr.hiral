Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At Dr. Hiral Homoeopathy, we are committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our patients' personal and clinical information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you access our wellness portal.

badge 1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide high-precision classical homeopathic treatment and secure patient services, including:

security_key 2. How We Protect Your Data

share 3. Sharing of Information

Your medical history, clinical records, and diagnostic details are treated as highly confidential. Data is strictly utilized for your clinical wellness mapping and treatment tracking.
